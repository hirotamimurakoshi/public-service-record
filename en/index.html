HIROTAMI MURAKOSHI

Public policy, government affairs, and administrative innovation

I work at the intersection of public policy, government, and business, drawing on experience in Japan’s national legislature, foreign affairs, municipal leadership, and public-sector advisory.

Experience

Led policy development, budget decisions, and administrative management in a Japanese municipality of approximately 500,000 residents. Initiatives included digital government, redesigned municipal services, public–private partnerships, and childcare capacity expansion.

Member of the House of Representatives / Parliamentary Vice-Minister for Foreign Affairs

Engaged in legislative policy, national government, and international affairs. As secretary-general of a cross-party parliamentary group, participated in the preparation of draft legislation concerning capital sentencing and criminal justice.

Public-sector advisory
